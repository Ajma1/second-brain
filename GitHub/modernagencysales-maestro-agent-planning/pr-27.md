# Onboarding v2 — Sandra texts live, draft center-stage, Talk Mode lands populated

PR #27 in modernagencysales/maestro-agent-planning — closed — by kimprobably — [https://github.com/modernagencysales/maestro-agent-planning/pull/27](https://github.com/modernagencysales/maestro-agent-planning/pull/27)

## Summary
Three changes that address every piece of your feedback + close the loop on what onboarding actually delivers.

### 1. Sandra texts you in real-time (the phone now has a job)

Every scrape milestone fires a tiny Haiku call with the actual scraped data. The phone gets opinionated, specific texts as work happens.

Real outputs from a fresh e2e run:
- *\"ok — calling your LinkedIn and reading your site now\"* (start)
- *\"seeing 4.4x ROAS as your hero metric—let's build messaging around that performance proof point.\"* (after website)
- *\"17k ads in 3 months is insane volume—how are you thinking about quality control at that scale?\"* (after profile)
- *\"your posts lead with pain, not vision—stronger if you showed the strategic *why* before the tool features.\"* (after posts — actual opinion!)
- *\"writing one for Monday now —\"* (after synth)
- *\"just finished your 'launch velocity' post—what do you think?\"* (after draft)

Cost: ~\$0.005 extra per run. Phone goes from prop → soul.

### 2. Draft is center-stage, not offside

Layout flipped to match Talk Mode's rule (asset = center):
\`\`\`
phone | LINKEDIN PREVIEW | live feed rail (280px)
\`\`\`

The LinkedIn preview **renders immediately** with placeholder author + a product-tour body explaining what's happening. Morphs to real content as data arrives. **No spinners ever.** The empty state IS the pitch.

Pitch body that fills the post box during the wait:
> Sandra's reading your LinkedIn right now. In a few seconds she'll have your voice down. Then she'll write a post for Monday — using your numbers, your proof points, your angle. You approve it or skip it. Nothing publishes without your green light…

### 3. Talk Mode lands populated, not empty

New \`seedPostOnboarding\` action fires in background after the focal draft completes:
- Generates 4 more posts (one Haiku call returning a JSON array of distinct angles)
- Seeds them as \`contentObjects\` spread across the next 2 weekdays
- Creates 1 \`cmoAsk\` pointing at Tuesday's draft (\"review Tuesday's draft\")
- Inserts 4 \`groundedNodes\` (brain) for the synthesized facts

When user lands in \`/talk\`: calendar populated, inbox has START HERE, brain panel shows real numbers. **The product is alive at minute 1.**

## E2E verification (Raphael + admanage.ai, fresh run)

## Tradeoffs / things I considered but skipped
- **Phone resize.** Considered shrinking it to 320px since the draft is now wider. Kept at 380 — the phone is the metaphor and Sandra is now *active*, so it earns its space.
- **A/B Haiku vs DeepSeek side-by-side.** Cool but separate scope. The \`model\` field on the run already supports it (just override the arg).
- **Real token streaming for the draft.** Still post-hoc chunked. Discussed earlier; deferred.
- **\"Onboarded\" flag on users table.** Skipped (would need schema migration). For the prototype, the form is always available; not worth a migration.
