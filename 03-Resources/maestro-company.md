# Maestro — Company & People

Research facet: the **company and people** behind Maestro / the GitHub org **modernagencysales** (marketing site https://maestrogtm.com, web app https://app.maestrogtm.com).

> Method note: Researched via WebFetch of real pages + WebSearch. The marketing site (modernagencysales.com) and the web app are client-rendered JavaScript apps, so several pages returned only a "Loading Maestro..." shell to WebFetch and could not be scraped for body text. The `/about`, `/contact` paths on maestrogtm.com returned HTTP 404 at time of research. **CRITICAL caution:** "Maestro" is an extremely crowded name — there are many unrelated companies (go-maestro.com PE platform, Maestro.io live-streaming, Maestro AI mortgage fintech, Maestro Group sales coaching, RunMaestro agent orchestration, etc.). None of those are this company. The Brazilian **maestroabm.com** is also a different company. Facts below were only accepted when tied specifically to **maestrogtm.com / modernagencysales / Tim Keen**.

## Summary (what this company is)

- **Name / product:** Maestro. Tagline on the marketing site: **"Turn expertise into pipeline."** (Source: https://maestrogtm.com)
- **What it is:** A go-to-market (GTM) platform. Per its own course/landing copy, "MaestroGTM was built from running LinkedIn GTM across **200+ accounts** and booking **5,000+ inbound calls**," now packaged as internal Maestro bootcamp material. (Source: search snippet of https://app.maestrogtm.com/p/tim-keen/ai-powered-prospecting-system)
- **Marketing site footer:** "© 2026 Maestro. All rights reserved." (Source: https://www.maestrogtm.com/integrations/apollo-io)
- **Predecessor offering:** "Modern Agency Sales" / a "LinkedIn Inbound Bootcamp" and an "agency sales course" appear to be earlier incarnations of the same body of work by the same person (Tim Keen) before/alongside the Maestro productization. (Sources: https://www.modernagencysales.com/, LinkedIn post history below)

#### Tim Keen — background / career timeline (from interview + LinkedIn)
Primary source: Hooked On Startups interview — https://hookedonstartups.com/tim-keen-digital-marketing-expert-and-co-founder-of-loop-club/
- Origin: Started in **Melbourne, Australia** → relocated to **Montreal, Canada** → later **Los Angeles, USA** → now **Brooklyn / New York** (current, per LinkedIn).
- Early entrepreneurship: dropshipping ventures; learned hands-on rather than via formal business education; background in writing and music.
- Worked at **MuteSix** (digital agency later "sold to Dentsu"); also referenced as a former **BarkBox** employee (Source: search result). Note: the MuteSix/BarkBox employment details are reported in secondary write-ups and are partially unverified.
- **Co-founder of Loop Club** (loop.club), a Shopify-focused growth/performance-marketing agency, founded during the COVID-19 pandemic (March 2020 onward). Loop Club is a **Shopify Plus Partner**. (Sources: hookedonstartups.com; https://www.loop.club/)
- Claimed traction at Loop Club: team collectively spent ~**$100M** on ads across clients; one client (Trophy Skin) scaled "$40,000/month → $1.2M/month in about five months." (Source: hookedonstartups.com)
- **"1x exit":** His own LinkedIn headline says "1x exit"; secondary sources say he "scaled and sold a $2m+ ecommerce agency in under 2 years" and "$0 to 7-figures in under 2 years." Exact buyer/date/figure are reported inconsistently across third-party pages — **treat the specific dollar amounts as unverified.** (Sources: https://www.linkedin.com/in/tim-keen/; search snippets)
