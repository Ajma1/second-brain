# Contea.ai

"The Operating System for LinkedIn Ghostwriters & Agencies." A centralized
platform that streamlines LinkedIn content creation: post ideation, client
collaboration, scheduling, and analytics — cutting fulfillment time so
ghostwriters serve more clients.

- **For:** LinkedIn ghostwriters, content agencies, corporate influencer
  managers, freelance creators.
- **Category:** ghostwriting operations platform.

## Key features
- AI post-idea generation with daily industry news.
- Client dashboard: content calendar + task management.
- Async, voice-driven client input/feedback.
- AI text engine for rapid drafting; client "avatar" customization.
- Automated scheduling to LinkedIn; performance analytics + client reporting.
- Podcast-to-post conversion.
