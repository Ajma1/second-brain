# feat: team member assignment and onboarding per persona

- **Identifier:** MAE-494
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-07-30
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-494/feat-team-member-assignment-and-onboarding-per-persona

## User need

Agency owners with multiple ghostwriters need to assign specific writers to specific client personas, and onboard new writers with all the voice context they need to start writing immediately.

**In their words:**

* "Consistency of quality across team members regardless of experience level"
* "Writing for all sales reps across multiple clients is unmanageable"
* "Getting writers to adopt AI tools is the current friction point"

## What Maestro has today

Workspace members exist, but there's no per-persona writer assignment or guided onboarding flow for new team members. When a new writer joins, there's no "here's everything you need to write for this client" experience.

## Proposed solution

* Per-persona writer assignment (this writer handles these 3 clients)
* Writer onboarding flow: auto-surface the client's Brain, voice brief, content rules, and example posts
* Role-scoped access so writers only see their assigned personas
* Quality comparison across writers for the same persona

* Adam: "getting writers to adopt AI tools is the current friction point"
* Nicole: manages 2 ghostwriters, best clients are coaching grads who "bring ideas but lack editorial eye"
