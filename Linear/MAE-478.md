## Outcome

Make the receipt-backed Inspiration fixture purge safe to deploy and later execute without deleting drifted rows, creating dangling references, accepting caller-asserted authority, or applying staging observation policy in production.

## Verified prior art

Recovered implementation is a strict 13-wave remote stack:

* `codex/inspiration-fixture-purge-wave-01-schema` at `152926024a710128e1f4985717d97c74e4bf97e4`

Independent integration review on 2026-07-18 found the stack not integration-ready. Do not merge or execute the current refs.

## Required corrections

1. Add a workspace mutation revision/fence spanning every paginated dependency audit page through quarantine and finalization. Every relevant writer must participate; destructive transitions require the pinned revision to remain unchanged transactionally.
2. Bind the reviewed manifest to canonical full live-row payload hashes for every target. Compare exact live payloads against reviewed/tombstoned payloads before irreversible deletion.
3. Replace caller-defined selector/SHA/free-form operator identity as authority with an approved deployment/operation receipt and exact deploy-pinned reviewed manifest hash. Store no credential material.
4. Prove complete dependency coverage, including duplicate stable retrieval chunk/recommendation IDs and nested `linkedinRetrievalMetadata` recipe-provenance references.
5. Make the observation window and environment applicability explicit policy/approved-operation data; fail closed outside the authorized environment.
6. Keep lifecycle capabilities internal and tenant-derived. Do not weaken OAuth, readiness, scanner, or serving gates.

## Acceptance

* Behavioral and adversarial tests prove writes after any audit page cannot be deleted through stale zero-count evidence.
* Any target-field drift after review or tombstoning blocks quarantine/finalization.
* An unapproved manifest, deployment, actor receipt, environment, or operation cannot enter a destructive transition.
* Duplicate/external/nested references block the purge.
* Every implementation PR is ≤300 changed production-source lines, links this Linear issue and its Fabro-approved bundle, and passes all required exact-SHA gates.
* Purge execution remains separately gated on deployed protocol, exact reviewed manifest, explicit operator authority, observation completion, and authenticated tenant-safe staging proof.
