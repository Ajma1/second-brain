# feat: client approval page with inline editing and track changes

- **Identifier:** MAE-493
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-08-01
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-493/feat-client-approval-page-with-inline-editing-and-track-changes

## User need

Ghostwriters need clients to review and approve individual posts with the ability to make inline edits. Current approval workflows are clunky — switching between tools, no track changes, can't send individual posts for review.

**In their words:**

* "no track changes, clunky approvals, can't send individual posts for review"
* "clients respond last-minute with changes"
* "Client review via Google Doc; one client edits and approves, another gives full autonomy"

## What Maestro has today

Shareable client approval page exists (no login needed) — but it appears to be view-only approve/reject. Clients can't edit inline or leave comments on specific parts of the draft.

## Proposed solution

Extend the approval page with:

* Inline text editing (track changes style)
* Per-paragraph commenting
* Change diff view showing what the client modified
* Option for "full autonomy" clients to skip review entirely

## ICP evidence

* Nicole: "no track changes, clunky approvals"
* Heri: "clients respond last-minute with changes"
* Muhammad: one client edits and approves, another gives full autonomy — need to support both modes
