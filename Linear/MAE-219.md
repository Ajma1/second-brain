## Key Constraint

All Braintrust model-level wrappers (`wrapAISDKModel`, `BraintrustMiddleware`, `wrapOpenRouter`) are v1/v2 only and **silently no-op or fail to compile** on `ai@6` that Convex Agent mandates. Capture must go through Agent's post-call hooks instead. The design doc explains the full pattern:

* Durable transactional outbox (workpool delivery)
* Deterministic-id idempotent upsert
* Deletion-aware claim
* Atomic scorer lease
