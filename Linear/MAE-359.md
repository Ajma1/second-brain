# Video platform integration — ingest video source material into content workflow

- **Identifier:** MAE-359
- **Status:** In Review
- **Assignee:** Tim Keen
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-20
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-359/video-platform-integration-ingest-video-source-material-into-content

## User Story

**As a LinkedIn ghostwriter, I want to connect video source material (e.g. from [Frame.io](<http://Frame.io>)) to the content creation workflow, so that transcripts flow into Maestro automatically without manual copy-paste.**

## Source

Dalton Danks call (2026-06-18). Dalton's workflow starts with video → editor cuts video → Dalton manually takes transcript → drops into AI tool. Videos currently live on [Frame.io](<http://Frame.io>).

> "we make like a first pass cuts on the video to clean up… and then put that into [Claude]"

## Gap from Existing Issues

[MAE-258](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-258/e4-3-built-in-transcription-no-separate-transcription-tool-in-the) (built-in transcription) covers audio/video transcription. [MAE-261](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-261/e4-6-slacknotiondrive-integration-client-knowledge-flows-in) (Slack/Notion/Drive integration) covers knowledge integrations. Neither addresses connecting a video hosting/review platform as a source material feed.

## Prior Art

**maestro-v2-3**:

* `postFormat` includes `video_script` — text content type for repurposing, not video hosting
* `mediaAssetKind` includes `video` in storage layer
* `knowledgeSourceKind` has no video platform provider
* Product Scope Doc puts "Podcast hosting / full audio platform" out of scope

**mas-platform**:

* `lib/storage/post-videos.ts` — Supabase Storage for video uploads (200MB, mp4/mov)
* `components/content-pipeline/VideoDropZone.tsx` — drag-and-drop upload with progress
* `trigger/publish-video.ts` — video publish worker to LinkedIn via Unipile
* `components/funnel/public/VideoEmbed.tsx` — YouTube/Vimeo/Loom URL-to-iframe
* No [Frame.io](<http://Frame.io>) or third-party video platform connector

## Implementation Notes

[Frame.io](<http://Frame.io>) has a REST API with webhooks for asset events. The integration could:

1. Watch for "asset ready" webhooks when a video edit is complete
2. Pull the video and auto-transcribe ([MAE-258](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-258/e4-3-built-in-transcription-no-separate-transcription-tool-in-the))
3. Route the transcript to the client's content workflow ([MAE-256](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-256/e4-1-transcript-intake-auto-route-to-client-and-trigger-content))

This closes the gap between the video editing process and content creation. Could also support Loom, Riverside, Descript as alternative sources since Dalton's clients may use different tools. Start with a generic "paste video URL or upload" flow, add [Frame.io](<http://Frame.io>) webhook integration later.
