# [ACPF-8] Draft review, approval, and optional scheduling lifecycle

- **Identifier:** MAE-316
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-17
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-316/acpf-8-draft-review-approval-and-optional-scheduling-lifecycle

## Goal

Define the v1 post lifecycle where the agency edits until approved, and approved means ready to post.

## Scope

* Align the lifecycle with the existing clean-repo piece status model.
* Support editing/revising drafts until approved.
* Implement small capabilities such as `listDrafts`, `reviseDraft`, `approveDraft`, and `scheduleApprovedPost`.
* Approved means ready to post.
* Schedule date/time is optional; if provided, move to scheduled.
* Keep actual publishing and LinkedIn posting out of scope.
* Ensure Kanban/status model is consistent across UI, CLI, and backend.

## Acceptance

* Agency can keep editing a draft until it is approved.
* Approved drafts are visible as ready to post.
* Scheduled drafts include schedule date/time.
* No changes-requested/client-approval state is introduced in this slice.
