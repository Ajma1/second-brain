## Goal

Perform the human/product review that decides whether the pilot recommendations are actually useful enough to move beyond shadow/review mode.

## Inputs

Use the pilot artifacts from Task 17, especially:

* `beneficial_adjacency_review_queue.csv`
* `beneficial_adjacency_recommendations.csv`
* `beneficial_adjacency_eval_summary.json`
* `adversarial_review.csv`
* rendered gallery output from Task 16 if available

## Work

Review a representative set of recommendations and label them with:

* `useful`
* `obvious`
* `spurious`
* `unsafe`
* `needs_more_evidence`

Check specifically:

* Does the recommendation tell an operator what to study or do?
* Is it beneficial adjacency rather than generic similarity?
* Is evidence sufficient and inspectable?
* Are lead magnets separated from organic templates?
* Are hook/template features useful without making generation too rigid?
* Are any raw third-party actor display fields exposed in default views?
* Do recommendations beat simple baselines/negative controls by human judgment?

## Constraints

* Do not mark the pilot `decision` ready if unsafe labels exist, evidence is missing for high-confidence rows, or recommendations do not beat baselines.
* Treat raw engagement actors as analytics-only unless a later product/privacy decision changes that.

## Acceptance Criteria

* Review labels are saved in the expected review artifact or summarized in a follow-up issue/comment.
* Decision is recorded as `shadow`, `review`, or `decision` with rationale.
* Follow-up issues are opened for any recurring failure modes or expansion opportunities.
