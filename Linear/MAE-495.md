# feat: prospecting pipeline for ghostwriters (find → qualify → outreach)

- **Identifier:** MAE-495
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-07-30
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-495/feat-prospecting-pipeline-for-ghostwriters-find-qualify-outreach

## User need

Prospecting is the [#1](<https://github.com/modernagencysales/maestro/issues/1>) time sink outside of content creation for ghostwriters. They manually search for potential clients, qualify them, and send personalized outreach — all without tooling.

**In their words:**

* "Prospecting is the primary time sink — manual search, qualification, personalized outreach"
* "Outbound activity not converting to discovery calls"
* "LinkedIn is most active social network but lowest ROI"

## What Maestro has today

* `leads.prepareAudience` workflow exists
* LinkedIn connection invites (`linkedin.connectionInvites.create`)
* Lead capture from lead magnets (`leads.capturedLeads.list`)

But there's no full prospecting pipeline: find prospects → qualify → personalized outreach → track conversion.

## Proposed solution

End-to-end prospecting workflow:

1. Define ICP criteria (industry, role, company size)
2. Find matching prospects on LinkedIn
3. Qualify against criteria automatically
4. Generate personalized outreach (using the ghostwriter's voice)
5. Track response rates and conversion to discovery calls
