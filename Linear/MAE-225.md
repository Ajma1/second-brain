## Prior art (verified 2026-06-12)

**Source:** maestro-v2-3 `packages/convex/convex/` — the bugs live in origin/main; the fixes are targeted patches to exact locations.

**Exact bug locations (from audit report** `04-audit-ajmals-day.md`**):**

* `agents/orchestrator.ts:≈601` — `sendCoPilotMessage` (no auth guard, public mutation)
* `agents/orchestrator.ts:≈614` — `sendCoPilotVoiceMessage` (no auth guard, public mutation)
* `agents/content.ts:≈293` — `draftPost` (no auth guard, exposes full content pipeline)
* `agents/email.ts` — all functions have zero auth guards
* `agents/orchestrator.ts:≈975` — `assertSelfWhenAuthenticated` is fail-open (returns without throwing when auth missing)
* `lib/auth/requireUserAccess.ts:≈84` — `assertSelfUserUnlessDemo` bypasses all auth checks when `MAESTRO_DEMO_MODE=true`
* `brain/positionSynthesis.ts:≈276` — `positionRagKey` assembled without tenant slug, cross-client contamination risk

**Auth wrappers to apply (PORT-AS-IS from** `lib/auth/`**):**

* `lib/auth/requireUserAccess.ts` — `requireUserAccess` (the correct auth-first pattern)
* `lib/auth/wrappers.ts` — `workspaceQuery`, `workspaceMutation`, `workspaceAction`
* `lib/auth/requireWorkspaceAccess.ts` — workspace boundary enforcement

**Salvage verdict:** REBUILD-AS-SPEC — these are targeted fixes, not ports. Apply the existing `requireUserAccess`/`workspaceMutation` wrappers to the four unguarded functions; flip `assertSelfWhenAuthenticated` to throw; remove the `MAESTRO_DEMO_MODE` bypass; migrate old positionRagKey format in a one-time backfill.
