# Audience-quality engagement signals — ICP scoring of post engagers

- **Identifier:** MAE-356
- **Status:** In Progress
- **Assignee:** Tim Keen
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-20
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-356/audience-quality-engagement-signals-icp-scoring-of-post-engagers

## User Story

**As a LinkedIn ghostwriter, I want to understand whether my client's content is attracting the right audience (not just any engagement), so that I can optimize content strategy for quality leads rather than vanity metrics.**

## Source

Dalton Danks call (2026-06-18). Dalton explicitly deprioritizes vanity metrics in favor of audience quality.

> "none of it actually matters besides say profile views from the target audience"

**Constraint:** LinkedIn API does not provide profile views, saves, or DM shares. Audience quality must be derived from available signals — primarily who comments and reacts (their profiles can be enriched and ICP-scored).

## Gap from Existing Issues

[MAE-286](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-286/e9-1-watchlist-outlier-scanning-relative-outliers-vs-accounts-own) (outlier scanning) and [MAE-252](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-252/e3-1-attribution-which-postmagnetemail-drove-each-lead-and-booked-call) (attribution) segment by content type, not by audience quality. No existing issue addresses "did this post attract the **right** people."

## Prior Art

**mas-platform**:

* `engagementEvents` table tracks event type + post_id but no audience/segment metadata
* `AudienceFilterBuilder.tsx` segments email subscribers — wrong domain but similar UX patterns
* Performance analyzer segments by content attributes (type, pillar, hook) not audience attributes

**maestro-v2-3**:

* `engagementEvents` has `provider: "linkedin_api" | "connectsafely"` and event types including profile_view, comment, reaction
* `engagementResolutions` table resolves events to leads — closest to what's needed

## Implementation Notes

Approach: when someone comments or reacts, enrich their LinkedIn profile (title, company, industry) and ICP-score them against the client's target audience. Aggregate per-post: "60% of engagers matched ICP" vs "20% matched." This gives the ghostwriter a content-quality signal beyond impressions. Depends on LinkedIn analytics ingestion story and a profile enrichment capability.
