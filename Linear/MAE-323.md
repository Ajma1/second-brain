## Goal

Capture the open decision on how v1 should define niche context for Maestro-owned market intelligence.

## Status

Waiting approval from Tim.

## Decision needed

Choose the v1 niche model for market intelligence reads and refresh jobs:

* Free-form niche text per client/agency.
* Maestro-admin controlled niche taxonomy.
* Hybrid: free-form query mapped to an admin-controlled `nicheKey`.

## Implementation guidance while waiting

* Use `nicheKey`/`nicheQuery` abstractions in schema and capability contracts.
* Do not hard-code a final taxonomy before this decision lands.
* Market intelligence capabilities should remain callable by client/persona/niche without needing a rewrite later.

## Acceptance

* Tim-approved v1 niche model is documented.
* Follow-up implementation issues are adjusted only if the decision changes schema/capability details.
