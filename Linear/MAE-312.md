# [ACPF-4] Client voice and positioning summary capabilities

- **Identifier:** MAE-312
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-20
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-312/acpf-4-client-voice-and-positioning-summary-capabilities

## Goal

Turn Harvest artifacts into reusable client-persona context for idea generation and drafting.

## Scope

* Implement small capabilities such as `summarizeClientVoice`, `summarizeClientPositioning`, `detectClientContentPillars`, and `detectClientBestPerformingPosts`.
* Use profile, posts, and comments/replies evidence from Harvest artifacts.
* Store structured voice/persona outputs with provenance and model/run metadata.
* Capture best-performing or most useful posts/ideas as evidence artifacts.
* Make summaries queryable by workflows, UI, CLI, and admin.

## Acceptance

* Voice/persona summaries can be regenerated from source artifacts.
* Idea generation and drafting can resolve the current summary without re-reading raw Harvest payloads every time.
* Outputs include enough evidence to explain why ideas fit the client.
