# [E2/E1] Generic document surface primitive for editable Notion-style pages

- **Identifier:** MAE-403
- **Status:** In Progress
- **Assignee:** Tim Keen
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-28
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-403/e2e1-generic-document-surface-primitive-for-editable-notion-style

## Context

Lead magnets need reusable editable landing pages, thank-you pages, and delivered assets. Studio already has a BlockNote/ProsemirrorSync editor, annotations, accept/reject suggestions, version rows, and a process rail, but those contracts are hard-wired to pieceId and draftVersions.

## Outcome

Design and implement a reusable document platform that supports full-page and embedded BlockNote surfaces, generic document identity, durable snapshots/revisions, human/AI markup with accept/reject, source/process rails, and markdown export. Studio pieces should become one consumer of the primitive; lead magnet asset/landing/thank-you pages should become documents rather than bespoke payload blobs.

## Scope notes

* Quiz/calculator builders are out of scope for this stack.
* Preserve [MAE-249](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-249/e2-2-lead-magnet-opt-in-funnel-delivery-by-email-with-evidence-trail) lead magnet funnel/delivery ledger work; document pages plug into funnels but do not replace opt-in/delivery evidence.
* First slice is a design/spec pass after deep repo analysis.
* Implementation should be a stack of small PRs and should remove the long-term pieceId dependency from editor primitives rather than adding another piece-only surface.
