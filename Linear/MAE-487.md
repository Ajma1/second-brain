# bug: research completes but Brain pages are empty — analysis not populating

- **Identifier:** MAE-487
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Assignee:** —

## Problem

LinkedIn research scrapes artifacts and posts successfully (100 artifacts, 96 posts for Tim Keen), but the analysis step does not populate Brain with structured pages, concepts, or voice evidence. Brain shows only empty "Untitled" pages and "No active approved corpus" under Inspiration.

This has been happening for at least a week — not related to recent staging deploy issues.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Create a persona with a LinkedIn URL
2. Click "Start research" on the Research & Voice page
3. Research runs: scrapes artifacts and posts successfully
4. "Analysis running" badge appears briefly, then flips back to "Research ready"
5. Go to Brain → only empty "Untitled" pages, no extracted evidence

## Expected behavior

After research completes, Brain should contain:

* Pages with extracted voice patterns, topics, and writing style
* Concepts linked from the persona's content
* Source evidence for voice brief generation

## What works

* LinkedIn scraping (artifacts + posts captured)
* Research status tracking (badges update correctly)

## What doesn't work

* Analysis → Brain population (the step that turns raw scraped data into structured pages)
* Likely the LLM calls that process scraped content are failing silently
