## Context

The **Methodology Platform** is the centerpiece: a ghostwriter encodes their method **once** (mix, cadence, templates, rules, voice) → it's applied across **every client in their own protected voice** → it improves via a **versioned, opt-in** loop. Pass 1 (the `/methodology` page — the Builder + the A/B proof) is built, deployed, and on this branch. The exhaustive technical reference is `docs/COCKPIT-BUILD-REFERENCE.md`.
