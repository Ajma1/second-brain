# Call capture → business intelligence — extract deal signals, ICP patterns, and competitive intel

- **Identifier:** MAE-362
- **Status:** Done
- **Assignee:** Tim Keen
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-24
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-362/call-capture-business-intelligence-extract-deal-signals-icp-patterns

## User Story

**As an agency owner, I want every prospect and client call automatically analyzed for business intelligence — deal signals, ICP validation, competitive mentions, pricing sensitivity, and churn risk — so that patterns across all conversations inform my business strategy without manual note-taking.**

## Source

Dalton Danks call (2026-06-18). A single prospect call reveals: revenue ($8k), client count (4), pricing ($2k/client), growth target (double in 3-6 months), team size (2), ICP (consultants targeting mid-market/enterprise), acquisition channels (mostly inbound), retention (6+ months), competitive tools (Claude, Canva, [Frame.io](<http://Frame.io>), Notion), and specific bottlenecks (fulfillment time blocking growth). This intelligence is currently lost after the call unless manually captured.

## Capability

When a call recording lands in Maestro (via maestro-capture):

1. Transcript is processed and classified by call type (prospect discovery, client check-in, onboarding, etc.)
2. An agent extracts structured business intelligence:
   * **Deal signals** — budget, timeline, decision-making process, buying triggers
   * **ICP data** — role, company size, industry, current stack, pain severity
   * **Competitive intel** — tools mentioned, what they like/dislike about alternatives
   * **Pricing signals** — reactions to pricing, value perception, ROI expectations
   * **Churn risk** (for existing clients) — frustration signals, unmet expectations
   * **Expansion signals** — interest in additional services, referral potential
3. Structured data is stored in the brain with call attribution
4. Cross-call dashboards surface patterns: "80% of prospects use Claude Projects", "average prospect revenue is $8-15k"

## Integration Points

* maestro-capture → Maestro brain (transcript ingestion)
* Brain → CRM enrichment (deal signals to pipeline)
* Brain → analytics (cross-call pattern detection)
* Brain → product discovery pipeline (overlaps with product discovery story for prospect calls)

## Implementation Notes

This is the most ambitious of the three call capture downstream paths. Start with structured extraction into the brain. The cross-call analytics and CRM integration can layer on top. The key differentiator from tools like Gong/Chorus is that this intelligence feeds back into the content and product strategy — it's not isolated in a sales tool.
