# feat: LinkedIn engagement assistance (suggested comments)

- **Identifier:** MAE-497
- **Status:** Done
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-08-01
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-497/feat-linkedin-engagement-assistance-suggested-comments

## User need

LinkedIn commenting and engagement is a massive time sink for ghostwriters and their clients. Some refuse to outsource it entirely, but all want help.

**In their words:**

* "Humanizes all AI output manually; will not outsource posting or commenting to anyone"
* "LinkedIn is most active social network but lowest ROI"

## What Maestro has today

No commenting or engagement features. The engagement capture page (`/engagement`) exists for tracking but not for assisting with responses.

## Proposed solution

"Suggested comments" — not auto-commenting, but voice-matched comment drafts the writer can review and edit:

* Surface posts from the client's network worth engaging with
* Generate comment drafts in the client's voice
* Writer reviews, edits, and posts manually
* Track which comments led to profile visits or connections

This respects the "will not outsource commenting" stance while reducing the time investment.

## ICP evidence

* Jon Keel: "humanizes all AI output manually; will not outsource posting or commenting to anyone"
* David Stone: "LinkedIn is most active social network but lowest ROI"
* General: engagement is how ghostwriters justify their value beyond just writing posts
