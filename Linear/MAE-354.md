# Production-ready AI image generation for LinkedIn posts

- **Identifier:** MAE-354
- **Status:** Done
- **Assignee:** Tim Keen
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-25
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-354/production-ready-ai-image-generation-for-linkedin-posts

## User Story

**As a LinkedIn ghostwriter, I want AI-generated images for posts that are truly production-ready and brand-consistent, so that I don't need a separate designer or Canva to finish every image.**

## Source

Dalton Danks call (2026-06-18). Dalton does ~4 image posts out of 12 per month. His wife/designer handles images manually in Canva. He tried AI tools but they get to 90% — the last 10% always requires manual work.

> "it would get like 90% of the way… was like almost impossible. So, like, let's just rebuild this in Canva. Like, it'll be faster."

## Gap from Existing Issues

[MAE-273](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-273/e6-7-visual-asset-generation-carousels-and-animated-infographics-in) (visual asset generation) covers carousels and animated infographics in the pipeline. It does not address single-image generation for posts (branded quote cards, text-over-image graphics) or the specific quality bar problem where AI gets close but not close enough.

## Prior Art (reference for concepts, not code to port)

**maestro-v2-3**:

* Schema has `image_generation` usage event kind and `mediaAssets` table with `kind: "image"` — placeholders only
* Product Scope Doc explicitly puts "Full design tool (Canva competitor)" out of scope: "Integrate with Canva. Don't rebuild it."
* No image generation provider integration

**mas-platform** (basic implementation):

* `server/services/image-generate.service.ts` — DALL-E 3, BYOK, three sizes, Helicone proxy
* `components/posts/editor/GenerateImageDialog.tsx` — simple prompt textarea, no style picker
* Brand kit system exists (`api/resolve-brand-kit.ts` — colors, fonts, logo) but NOT connected to image generation prompts
* Carousel slide templates with brand-kit colors/fonts work — but carousel publishing blocked
* ScreenshotOne for lead magnet thumbnails

## Implementation Notes

Two paths: (1) integrate brand kit into image generation prompts (auto-construct prompts from post content + brand identity + style), or (2) focus on template-based graphics (quote cards, stat graphics, text overlays) that use brand kit directly rather than AI image gen. Path 2 may be more reliable for the "last 10%" problem since it's deterministic. Consider both — AI gen for complex images, templates for branded graphics.
