# feat: per-persona cost tracking and usage attribution

- **Identifier:** MAE-498
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-07-30
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-498/feat-per-persona-cost-tracking-and-usage-attribution

## User need

Ghostwriters charge per client. They need to know how much each client costs them in AI usage to price their services correctly.

## What Maestro has today

Billing exists at the workspace level. The Research & Voice page shows cost per persona (e.g., "$0.02 used"). But there's no aggregated view across all personas showing total cost per client over time.

## Proposed solution

Per-persona usage dashboard:

* AI generation cost per persona per month
* Number of posts generated per persona
* Research/harvest cost per persona
* Total cost breakdown so ghostwriters can set pricing per client

## ICP evidence

* Shelley: 25 clients — needs to understand cost per client at scale
* Heri: 11 clients, manages a team — cost visibility across team members and clients
* General: ghostwriters pricing per client need to understand their own costs
