# Call capture → content pipeline — extract insights, quotes, and angles for client content

- **Identifier:** MAE-361
- **Status:** In Progress
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-20
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-361/call-capture-content-pipeline-extract-insights-quotes-and-angles-for

## User Story

**As a LinkedIn ghostwriter, I want call recordings automatically processed into content-ready material — quotable insights, strong opinions, story angles, and half-formed ideas worth developing — so that every client conversation feeds the content pipeline without manual mining.**

## Source

Dalton Danks call (2026-06-18). Dalton's entire content workflow starts with video recordings of client calls/thoughts. The manual step of watching video, pulling transcript, and identifying the "main idea" is where most time is spent.

> "we make like a first pass cuts on the video to clean up engines or just like dead space for that idea. And then put that into [Claude]. Because one of the issues that I was seeing before is if I give it too much of like the dead space or tangents, it has differentiated like what is really the key idea even if I prompted to do so."

## Capability

When a call recording lands in Maestro (via maestro-capture):

1. Transcript is processed with speaker diarization and dead-space removal
2. An agent extracts content-grade material:
   * **Quotable insights** — strong, specific statements worth building a post around
   * **Contrarian takes** — opinions that challenge conventional wisdom
   * **Story moments** — anecdotes, client wins, lessons learned
   * **Half-formed ideas** — interesting threads that need development
   * **Data points** — specific numbers, results, timelines
3. Each extract is attributed to the speaker with timestamp and exact quote
4. Extracts are routed to the client's idea bank ([MAE-313](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-313/acpf-5-idea-bank-schema-plus-search-and-selection-capabilities)) with suggested post formats
5. The ghostwriter reviews and promotes ideas to the content queue

## Integration Points

* maestro-capture → Maestro brain (transcript ingestion with speaker labels)
* Brain → idea bank (content-grade extracts as idea candidates)
* Idea bank → content pipeline (promoted ideas become draft briefs)

## Implementation Notes

This is the core value loop for ghostwriters — the call IS the source material. The key insight from Dalton's workflow is that raw transcripts have too much noise (tangents, dead space) and the AI struggles to identify what's actually worth writing about. The extraction agent needs to be opinionated about what constitutes a "post-worthy" insight vs. filler. Ties directly to [MAE-256](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-256/e4-1-transcript-intake-auto-route-to-client-and-trigger-content) (transcript intake) and [MAE-313](https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-313/acpf-5-idea-bank-schema-plus-search-and-selection-capabilities) (idea bank).
