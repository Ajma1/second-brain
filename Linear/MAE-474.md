# Activate legacy Blueprint recipients in Maestro

- **Identifier:** MAE-474
- **Status:** In Progress
- **Assignee:** Tim Keen
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-07-12
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-474/activate-legacy-blueprint-recipients-in-maestro

## Outcome

Turn the previously delivered Blueprint cohort into safely staged Maestro onboarding packages so recipients arrive with their profile context, lead-magnet ideas, and draft posts already present, while preserving verified identity and tenant ownership.

## Product intent

The legacy Blueprint generated a profile rewrite, three lead-magnet ideas, and approximately 60 draft posts per recipient. Reuse that completed work as a high-intent activation path inside Maestro rather than recreating Blueprint as a product.

## Guardrails

* Never fabricate WorkOS-backed Maestro users.
* Stage packages first; create the local user only after verified authentication.
* Attach a staged package through a verified invitation/claim contract.
* Keep imported drafts review-blocked and unschedulable until required context, provenance, lint, and human review pass.
* Keep all customer data out of shared dev and use synthetic staging canaries unless a real canary is explicitly approved.
* Do not use deploy keys pasted in chat; rotate them before rollout and use BWS-backed credentials.
* Do not recreate Blueprint routes, admin UI, or legacy schema in Maestro.

## Program acceptance

* Fresh, privacy-safe source inventory and exclusion report.
* Idempotent export/normalization into Maestro onboarding packages.
* Stable source receipts and per-record reconciliation.
* Persona-linked imports preserving legacy readiness and repair metadata.
* Verified invitation/claim and managed-workspace activation path.
* CMO completion requirements that cannot approve or schedule incomplete imported drafts.
* Synthetic staging canary, approved production canary, rollback rehearsal, and final cohort reconciliation.
* No duplicate posts, cross-tenant attachment, invented users, or silent scheduling.
