# 2.1 · Claim schema + grounding integration

MAE-31 — Backlog — priority None — team Maestro V2 — by Tim Keen
https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-31/21-claim-schema-grounding-integration

**Why.** Claims *are* the brain — one cited fact each. The key design choice is **provenance**: every claim is tagged `client` (the client's own words), `market` (what buyers/competitors say), or `internal` (what the product learned). That tag lets a generator reason about *whose* knowledge a fact is — ground the offer in `client`, ground a customer-language hook in `market`.
