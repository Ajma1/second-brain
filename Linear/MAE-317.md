# [ACPF-9] Agency methodology policy areas

- **Identifier:** MAE-317
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-17
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-317/acpf-9-agency-methodology-policy-areas

## Goal

Model agency methodology as independently resolvable policy areas, not one giant settings object.

## Scope

* Define policy-area schema and capability contracts.
* Include at least these policy areas: `hook_rules`, `angle_rules`, `voice_rules`, `content_formats`, `approval_rubric`, `cadence`, `banned_phrases`, `cta_rules`, `idea_scoring`, `drafting_style`, and `client_overrides`.
* Add read/write capabilities for policy areas.
* Make workflows resolve only the policy areas they need.
* Support agency defaults and per-client overrides.

## Acceptance

* Idea generation can resolve scoring, hook, angle, format, and client override policies independently.
* Drafting can resolve voice, CTA, banned phrases, drafting style, and approval rubric independently.
* Updating one policy area does not require rewriting unrelated methodology data.
