## Goal

Make recommendations reviewable and measurable before they are considered product-ready.

## Files

Modify:

* `tooling/corpus/beneficial-adjacency/eval-harness.mts`
* `tooling/corpus/linkedin-beneficial-adjacency.mts`
* `tooling/corpus/linkedin-beneficial-adjacency.test.mts`

## Work

Implement review/eval artifacts:

* `beneficial_adjacency_review_queue.csv`
* `beneficial_adjacency_eval_summary.json`
* `adversarial_review.csv`
* `beneficial_adjacency_examples.csv`

Review queue should mix real recommendation rows with negative controls such as random, popularity-only, same-cluster-nearest, and template-only baselines. Summary should include useful/obvious/spurious/unsafe/needs-more-evidence counts when labels are present and readiness tier calculation.

## Constraints

* Default readiness should be `shadow` or `review`; `decision` requires checks to pass.
* High-confidence rows require evidence.
* Unsafe labels or privacy failures block decision readiness.
* Rows without evidence must not render as normal high-confidence recommendations.
