# [ACPF-10] Maestro market intelligence refresh and read capabilities

- **Identifier:** MAE-318
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Assignee:** —
- **Team:** MAE
- **Updated:** 2026-06-17
- **URL:** https://linear.app/modern-agency-sales/issue/MAE-318/acpf-10-maestro-market-intelligence-refresh-and-read-capabilities

## Goal

Create the Maestro-owned market intelligence data product that workflows can query through small read capabilities.

## Scope

* Add schema for Maestro-owned trending posts, trending themes, trending ideas, performing posts, and performing patterns.
* Implement refresh capabilities/jobs such as `refreshTrendingPosts`, `refreshTrendingThemes`, `refreshTrendingIdeas`, and `refreshPerformingPatterns`.
* Implement small read capabilities such as `getTrendingPosts`, `getTrendingThemes`, `getTrendingIdeas`, `getPerformingPosts`, `getPerformingPatterns`, and `getNicheMarketSnapshot`.
* Store provenance, refreshedAt, nicheKey/nicheQuery, source metadata, and confidence/quality scores where applicable.
* Keep workflows reading from Convex-stored intelligence rather than doing broad external research inline.

## Acceptance

* Idea generation and drafting can call read capabilities for market context.
* Refresh jobs are separate from per-client drafting workflows.
* Niche lookup is abstracted behind `nicheKey`/`nicheQuery` pending Tim's decision.
